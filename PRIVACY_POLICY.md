## Privacy Policy

Dwarves Foundation built the Hidden Bar app as an Open Source app. This app is provided by Dwarves Foundation at no cost and is intended for use as is.

**Information Collection and Use**

The app does NOT use any third party services that may collect information used to identify you.

**Local Data Storage**

The app stores user preferences (such as auto-hide settings, keyboard shortcuts, and display options) locally on your device using macOS UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device and is not shared with any third party.

**Accessibility and Event Monitoring**

The app uses macOS global event monitoring (`NSEvent.addGlobalMonitorForEvents`) to detect mouse clicks outside the app, which is used to automatically collapse the menu bar. This monitoring is limited to detecting click events and does not record, store, or transmit any user activity.

**Contact Us**

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at macos@d.foundation.
